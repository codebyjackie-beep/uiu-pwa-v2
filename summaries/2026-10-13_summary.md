**"reference_pack 頂上係主因" is NOT supported by the data.**
- 8,353 listings: title-parsed 2,586, **no pack (unknown) 5,296 (63%)**, reference 471 (5.6%, 87 docs).
- Of the 145 doc-units with spread >5x, listings are 1,021 title / **6 reference** / 0 unknown; only 1 doc (`skewers`) has a reference listing involved at all.
- Reference listings carry `pack.unit` g (332) / ml (101) / each (38), but `computePerUnit` only aggregates `g_or_ml` / `each` -> g/ml reference listings are displayed with a per_unit but **never enter `per_unit_metric`**. Side effect: 35 docs with reference listings have metric AND count null (no price at all; none of them has public-recipe impact). So the reference fallback is effectively dead for g/ml; Guard 4 is a no-op for the aggregate unless the unit mismatch is deliberately fixed (decision for cloud).
- broth / stock `reference_pack` = null for all of: vegetable broth, chicken broth/stock, stock, vegetable or fish broth hot, chicken or fish broth. Their bad numbers come from:
  1. **n=1 / n=4 metrics** because 63% of listings have no parseable pack (e.g. `vegetable or fish broth, hot` = a single "Soup Broth Pack-Vegetable 150 g" £8.45 -> £0.0563/g).
  2. **Pack-parse bugs in the old regexes** (14 + 12 + 4 listings in the cache): `10x10g` / `4x400g` (no space, `` fails) not multiplied -> up to 10x too expensive (`Sainsbury's Stock Cubes Chicken 10x10g` = 10g -> £0.11/g, the x100 chicken stock spread); `10 Pack 100g` multiplied although 100g is already the total -> 10x too cheap (`Tesco Chicken Stock Cubes 10 Pack 100g` = 1000g -> £0.0011/g); `Pack of 6` not multiplied.
  3. **Wrong product / wrong price** (mixed peppers vs ground pepper, 6g parsley jar, dried mushrooms 30g, `Soup Broth Pack`, KTC 2.5kg at £0.52).
- cola (regular) x108: `Coca Cola 330ml Cans` £14.25 (a multipack with no count in the title) = £0.0432/ml vs `Kingsley Cola 2L` £0.0004; both are removed by a +-3x-of-median rule.
- chopped tomatoes x55.5: `KTC Chopped Tomatoes 2.5kg` £0.52 (£0.0002/g, price looks wrong) vs `Mutti Peeled 400g` £4.45.
- rice x19: title-only listings (reference listings are excluded from the metric); spread is real product variety (£0.52/kg own brand ... £11.5/5kg premium; `Tesco Easy Cook 500G` £4.75 looks like a mis-priced listing).
- **Doc-level +-3x-of-median outlier rule** brings 103 of the 145 spread doc-units to <=5x. 42 stay >5x (mushroom x5.2 after, spinach leaves, rice, carrot, mozzarella, ...) - mostly genuine product variety / dried-vs-fresh; `hold` will catch them against the current value.
